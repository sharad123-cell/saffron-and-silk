RANGPLAY SIMULATOR

Privacy notice

RangPlay is a local virtual-credit demo. It does not ask for an account, name, email address, phone number, payment details, or location.

Data and storage

Demo balance, picks, and round history exist only in the open browser tab and are cleared when the page is refreshed or closed. The app does not send this gameplay data to a server.

Network requests

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The stylesheet may request fonts from Google Fonts; those requests are handled under Google's privacy practices. The service worker caches the app shell on your device for offline loading.

Payments and prizes

RangPlay has no real-money deposits, withdrawals, purchases, cash-outs, or prizes. Virtual credits have no monetary value.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the GitHub repository hosting this demo.
