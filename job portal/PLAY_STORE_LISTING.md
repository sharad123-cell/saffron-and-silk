# RangPlay Play Store listing draft

## App name
RangPlay — Color Simulator

## Short description
A virtual-credit color simulator for exploring probability. No real money or prizes.

## Full description
Explore how chance works in RangPlay, a color-based probability simulator. Choose a color using fictional practice credits, watch a timed round resolve, and review recent outcomes.

RangPlay is for entertainment and learning only:

- Credits are fictional and have no monetary value.
- No deposits, payments, withdrawals, cash-outs, or prizes.
- Results are generated randomly; administrators cannot choose round results.
- Past results do not affect future rounds.

The app does not require an account and does not store gameplay history on a server. It is not a financial product and does not provide investment advice.
